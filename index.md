# Aolan Li, Ph.D.

<p class="hero-lead">Bayesian statistician working across statistical methodology, clinical trials, and collaborative biomedical research.</p>

I conduct collaborative statistical research at the Yale University School of
Nursing. I earned a Ph.D. in Statistics and an M.S. in Biostatistics from the
University of Connecticut. My work connects Bayesian model comparison and
Monte Carlo computation with longitudinal, multivariate, microbiome, and
multi-omics applications.
