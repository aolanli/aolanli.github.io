<p class="page-intro">
I develop and apply statistical methods for complex biomedical data, with a
particular interest in Bayesian model comparison, longitudinal analysis, and
reproducible collaborative research.
</p>

## Selected research areas

<div class="research-stack">
  <article class="research-panel">
    <p class="section-kicker">BAYESIAN METHODS</p>
    <h3>Model comparison and marginal likelihoods</h3>
    <p>
      My methodological work focuses on marginal-likelihood estimation for
      complex Bayesian models, including Monte Carlo strategies for practical
      model comparison. My dissertation,
      <em>Advancing Marginal Likelihoods for Longitudinal Data Analysis</em>,
      studied marginal-likelihood decomposition for covariance-structure
      assessment in repeated-measures randomized trials.
    </p>
    <p>
      <a href="https://doi.org/10.1002/wics.70058">Monte Carlo estimator comparison</a>
      <span aria-hidden="true"> · </span>
      <a href="https://doi.org/10.1093/sysbio/syad007">LoRaD</a>
    </p>
  </article>

  <article class="research-panel">
    <p class="section-kicker">LONGITUDINAL &amp; CLINICAL OUTCOMES</p>
    <h3>Developmental, behavioral, and patient-reported outcomes</h3>
    <p>
      I apply longitudinal, multivariate, and mediation methods to study how
      early-life exposures, relational contact, and patient-provider
      partnerships relate to neurodevelopment, behavioral development, pain,
      and quality of life. Applications span preterm-infant follow-up and
      patient-centered cancer research.
    </p>
    <p>
      <a href="https://doi.org/10.3389/fped.2025.1523952">Milk and neurodevelopment</a>
      <span aria-hidden="true"> · </span>
      <a href="https://doi.org/10.1188/25.ONF.448-459">Self-efficacy, pain, and quality of life</a>
      <span aria-hidden="true"> · </span>
      <a href="https://doi.org/10.1002/icd.70090">Relational contact and behavioral development</a>
    </p>
  </article>

  <article class="research-panel">
    <p class="section-kicker">BIOMEDICAL DATA SCIENCE</p>
    <h3>Microbiome, multi-omics, and health trajectories</h3>
    <p>
      At Yale, I collaborate on NIH-funded studies integrating microbiome and
      multi-omics analysis with longitudinal modeling and statistical machine
      learning to characterize biological pathways, response phenotypes, and
      self-management outcomes related to pain and other symptoms.
    </p>
    <p>
      <a href="https://doi.org/10.3389/frmbi.2026.1884540">Gut microbiota response phenotypes</a>
      <span aria-hidden="true"> · </span>
      <a href="https://doi.org/10.3390/biomedicines13092102">Multi-omics intervention study</a>
      <span aria-hidden="true"> · </span>
      <a href="https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.10.07.25337516">Machine-learning pain-response preprint</a>
    </p>
  </article>
</div>

## Methods and computing

<div class="method-grid">
  <div>
    <h3>Bayesian computation and simulation</h3>
    <p>Bayesian inference, MCMC and Monte Carlo computation, marginal-likelihood estimation, model comparison, and simulation-based evaluation.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <h3>Longitudinal and multivariate methods</h3>
    <p>Repeated measures, LMM, GLMM, MMRM, latent class mixed models, trajectory analysis, covariance structures, missing data, and survival analysis.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <h3>Causal and structural methods</h3>
    <p>Causal mediation, structural equation modeling, PCA and related dimension-reduction methods, regularization, and high-dimensional analysis.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <h3>Statistical machine learning</h3>
    <p>BART, random forests, XGBoost, clustering and trajectory phenotyping, cross-validation, and prediction-model assessment.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <h3>Microbiome and multi-omics</h3>
    <p>Diversity, ordination, PERMANOVA, DMM community modeling, differential abundance with ANCOM-BC2, ALDEx2, and LEfSe, longitudinal omics with dream, WGCNA, and pathway analysis.</p>
  </div>
  <div>
    <h3>Programming and reproducibility</h3>
    <p>R as the primary analysis language, with SAS, Stan, SQL, Python, R Markdown and Quarto, Git, and HPC/SLURM for reproducible statistical workflows.</p>
  </div>
</div>
